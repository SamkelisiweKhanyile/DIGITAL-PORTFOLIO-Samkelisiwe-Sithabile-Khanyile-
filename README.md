### STAR Reflection

**Situation:** During a collaborative group project, one team member consistently missed deadlines and failed to communicate their delays, causing frustration among the rest of the team.

**Task:** I needed to address the issue professionally while upholding **workplace etiquette** and ensuring the project stayed on track without creating an unnecessary conflict.

**Action:** Instead of confronting the individual in a public chat, I sent a private, objective message expressing concern and asking if I could help remove any roadblocks. I focused on the *impact* of the delay on the team's shared deadline, not on the individual's performance. I then documented the revised timeline and communicated it clearly to the whole group in a formal update email.

**Result:** The team member apologized and explained a personal issue, which we accommodated by adjusting their tasks. The professional approach resolved the conflict respectfully, maintained a positive working relationship, and ensured the project was submitted on time. This demonstrated my understanding of respectful, solution-focused communication in a professional environment.
