## Business Communication  

### Evidence  
*Professional Email Artefact*  

### STAR Reflection  
**Situation:** I needed to follow up with a potential internship host regarding an application, but I was uncertain about the appropriate professional tone and format for the communication.  

**Task:** I had to draft and send a concise, formal email inquiry that respected professional boundaries and clearly stated my intention.  

**Action:** I researched formal business email etiquette, focusing on a clear subject line, a professional greeting, and a structured body. I drafted the email using formal language, ensuring I clearly mentioned my name, the role I applied for, and a specific date for a follow-up call. I had my Work Readiness mentor review the draft before sending it.  

**Result:** The host responded promptly with a positive update on my application status. This experience reinforced the importance of clarity and professionalism in business communication, showing that a well-structured email can expedite professional processes and leave a positive impression.

## Interview Skills  

### Evidence  
*Interview Preparation Notes*  

### STAR Reflection  
**Situation:** I recognized that my nervousness and lack of structure in answering behavioral questions were weak points in my previous job search attempts.  

**Task:** I needed to develop a systematic approach to answering interview questions, particularly those requiring anecdotal evidence of problem-solving and teamwork.  

**Action:** I studied and practiced using the STAR technique to structure my answers. I identified three key project experiences (Vehicle Voting System, FlexHire, etc.) and prepared a scripted Situation, Task, Action, and Result for common questions like “Tell me about a challenge you overcame.”  

**Result:** During a mock interview, I was able to articulate my experiences clearly and confidently. The interviewer specifically praised my structured responses, which allowed me to showcase my skills effectively rather than rambling. This preparation has significantly boosted my confidence for real-world interviews.

## Mock Interview  

### Evidence  
*Mock Interview Feedback Form*  

### STAR Reflection  
**Situation:** I participated in a formal mock interview session hosted by the department to simulate a real industry setting for a Junior Developer role.  

**Task:** My task was to perform under pressure, answer both technical and behavioral questions, and receive constructive feedback on my overall presentation and technical knowledge.  

**Action:** I dressed in appropriate business attire, ensured my technical setup (camera, microphone) was professional, and reviewed my notes on the company and the role just before the interview. I maintained eye contact and used positive body language, focusing on delivering structured STAR responses.  

**Result:** While the feedback was generally positive on my composure and technical foundation (Java), the moderator noted that I needed to elaborate more on the impact of my project work (e.g., specific metrics). I immediately incorporated this feedback into my CV and future interview answers, ensuring I quantify my achievements. This demonstrated my ability to apply real-time feedback.

## Workplace Etiquette  

### Evidence  
*Workplace Etiquette Guidelines Summary*  

### STAR Reflection  
**Situation:** During a collaborative group project, one team member consistently missed deadlines and failed to communicate their delays, causing frustration among the rest of the team.  

**Task:** I needed to address the issue professionally while upholding workplace etiquette and ensuring the project stayed on track without creating an unnecessary conflict.  

**Action:** Instead of confronting the individual in a public chat, I sent a private, objective message expressing concern and asking if I could help remove any roadblocks. I focused on the impact of the delay on the team's shared deadline, not on the individual's performance. I then documented the revised timeline and communicated it clearly to the whole group in a formal update email.  

**Result:** The team member apologized and explained a personal issue, which we accommodated by adjusting their tasks. The professional approach resolved the conflict respectfully, maintained a positive working relationship, and ensured the project was submitted on time. This demonstrated my understanding of respectful, solution-focused communication in a professional environment.
